# v0.1b exit checklist

Roadmap exit: **a tester can take off, fly a pattern and land under every assist level;
input-to-control latency <= 25 ms p95; the take-off and landing ground-roll conformance points
deferred from v0.1a pass (`AC-3.2` complete).** (docs/roadmap.md, GDD 66.3 M1.) Closed only when
every item passes (AC-66.1). The gates are fixed in
[ADR-32](../adr/0032-ground-roll-conformance-method.md) and
[ADR-33](../adr/0033-v0-1b-gates.md), written before any gear or FCS code (AC-66.4).

## Known, outside this milestone

- WebKit in CI does not reach the running state in `browsers/boot.spec.ts`. This predates v0.1a and the
  job is informational.
- v0.1a item 13 (the owner's manual staging flight) was still pending when `v0.1a` was tagged.
- The owner's manual circuit per assist on staging (checklist in `docs/runbooks/reference-machines.md`)
  is requested and recorded when done; it does not block the tag.
- Gamepad and HOTAS were tested with stubbed Gamepad API objects only. The joystick profile is a guess at
  the usual layout; the binding editor is how a real device gets fixed.
- Informational misses and readings: the wet-runway landing rolls 569.8 m against C.7's 420 m; the
  circuit's C1 speed ratio is 1.404 at 8 710 kg (a robustness load, not the gate load); the 12 500 kg
  robustness load is above the aircraft's 11 500 kg maximum and so cannot be reached; mouse-aim under
  Full is informational; the stall speed is the same for take-off and landing flaps in the data.
- Not built, by decision: Auto-GCAS (the flag and the data block exist, the feature is off) and the
  GDD 11.18 autotuner. The gains in GDD 11.11 for the ramp and K were replaced by the incremental
  dynamic inversion (GDD 0.2.2).
- In CI the WebGPU shader path is validated by the shader compiler's front end (naga) only; the WebGPU
  reference runs drew frames with no console errors.
- Behaviour to know: the trim keys act only under Manual assist; the pause menu holds the sim; the
  g-effects dial applies at the next start.
- The reference runs used a local production preview of the final build rather than the Pages URL,
  because the deployed staging build lacked the speed-up of `6c1e851` (no hash changes); `staging-smoke`
  covers the deployed one.

## Recorded decisions (2026-10-22)

- `ldg_roll` keeps its 322 m target; the data is retuned (ADR-32). C.8's literal reading gives 332 m
  (+3.1 %).
- `AC-58.4` is scaled to the aircraft's turn rate (ADR-33) and gets a GDD 0.2.2 clarification.
- `AC-58.4`, 2026-10-24 (ADR-33 amendment 1): as first worded (the 0.5 s dwell over by `t_max`, throttle as trimmed) it passes 79 % and cannot pass 95 % for a target above the nose beyond 65 deg, because the aircraft bleeds energy in the pull-up. The owner chose: within 1 deg no later than `t_max` and then 0.5 s held, with the pilot holding full throttle. At the trimmed throttle the same reading gives 88.6 %.
- The virtual tester gates the exit; the owner's manual circuits do not block the tag.
- The owner can hand-test only keyboard and mouse.
