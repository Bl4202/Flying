# ADR-11: Original and fictional content

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Owner:** Bl4202
- **Source:** GDD §0.4 (imported verbatim at v0.0a)
- **Affects:** §12, §15, §37, §53

## Decision

**Aircraft, weapons, and factions are original/fictional designs** inspired by real performance classes. Geography is real. No real national flags, no real orders of battle, political borders hidden by default.

## Alternatives considered

Licensed real aircraft; real nations

## Rationale

Avoids trademark/licensing exposure and political sensitivity of simulating real-world conflicts over real territory; matches outline §70 ("factions abstract and fictional").
