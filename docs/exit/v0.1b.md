## Recorded decisions (2026-10-22)

- `ldg_roll` keeps its 322 m target; the data is retuned (ADR-32). C.8's literal reading gives 332 m
  (+3.1 %).
- `AC-58.4` is scaled to the aircraft's turn rate (ADR-33) and gets a GDD 0.2.2 clarification.
- `AC-58.4`, 2026-10-24 (ADR-33 amendment 1): as first worded (the 0.5 s dwell over by `t_max`, throttle as trimmed) it passes 79 % and cannot pass 95 % for a target above the nose beyond 65 deg, because the aircraft bleeds energy in the pull-up. The owner chose: within 1 deg no later than `t_max` and then 0.5 s held, with the pilot holding full throttle. At the trimmed throttle the same reading gives 88.6 %.
- The virtual tester gates the exit; the owner's manual circuits do not block the tag.
- The owner can hand-test only keyboard and mouse.
