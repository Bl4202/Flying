# v0.1d exit checklist

Roadmap exit: **G1 passes; ≥ 80 % of external playtesters take off, fly, shoot a bandit and land within
10 minutes (`AC-4.5` precursor).** (docs/roadmap.md, GDD 66.3 M1.) Closed only when every item passes
(AC-66.1). The gates are fixed in [ADR-45](../adr/0045-v0-1d-gates.md), written before any weapon, AI or
combat code (AC-66.4). The project is solo (OQ-02), so the bots gate the tag and the owner's own ratings
are recorded and do not block; the 12-tester blind study (G1(a)) is open as OQ-11
([docs/open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)).

## Found during the exit work, and fixed

- **The bandit's gun could not hit** (`5b47ca8`, ADR-47 amendment 2). GUN steered the bandit's nose onto its
  own gunsight's pipper, which moves with the nose, and fired whenever the nose was near it, wherever the
  player was: a Regular emptied its 150 rounds behind a player flying straight without a hit. No gate
  measured the bandit's hits (AI1 is position, AI8 firing). Found in the pre-merge walkthrough; a core test
  now holds it. BP2 fell from 42 and 43 to 32 and 33 because the bandit now shoots back.
- **Two gate tests had not been written** (the plan named them in the gate table and gave them to no work
  package): the NN-2 audit and turn test (`cdc00db`) and the DOM budget in a duel (`261d08b`).
- **AU5 timed out on CI** (71 s against 60 s): one view per test now (`443a537`).

## Disclosures

- **No browser test sees a missile launch.** Hands off the stick at the merge the seeker cannot lock (the
  Recruit turns past the 35° aspect before the bandit is inside the 2° field), and the bot seldom launches
  (6 missiles in 150 BP1 runs). The launch path is judged by CT2, MS1 to MS5 and the scene's tests (ADR-45
  amendment 2).
- The bot playtester is an instrument: it reads exact state and the HUD's cues. BP1 shows a pilot with
  perfect information can take off, kill a Recruit and land under each assist; it is not the playtesters'
  `AC-4.5` and does not stand for them.
- No GDD-spec Recommended machine was tested: the reference runs are the Mac M4 (ADR-28).

## Known, outside this milestone

- **G1(a)**, the blind panel of ≥ 12 testers, is deferred by the owner (OQ-11). v0.2 is not blocked by it.
- The bandit has no fuel logic: after about 6.5 minutes in afterburner it glides into the ground (ADR-47).
- No countermeasures, radar, RWR, DLZ table or component damage (GDD 0.2.4's clarifications list what v0.1d
  builds of each chapter).
